# OBTC Community Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Treat contributors, reviewers, users, and maintainers with respect. Technical
disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam,
and deliberate disclosure of another person's private information are not.

Keep criticism specific to ideas, code, evidence, and observable behavior.
Assume good faith while still requiring reproducible claims and careful review,
especially for consensus and fund-safety changes.

## Scope

This standard applies in OBTC repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Reporting

For a non-sensitive concern, open a concise issue without personal or secret
information. For a sensitive concern, open only a minimal issue asking for a
private contact channel; do not include the sensitive details publicly. A
report may be made from a GitHub identity that does not reveal a private legal
name.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its
participants. Reports will be evaluated on their evidence and impact, not on a
reporter's public identity.
